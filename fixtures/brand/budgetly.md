# Budgetly brand guide (fictional brand)

Budgetly is a budgeting app for people in their first years of earning their own money. It connects to your bank, sorts spending into categories automatically, and gives you a weekly "money check-in" with one suggestion. It is free, with an optional Plus tier.

## Voice
- Friendly, direct, a little dry. A smart older sibling, not a bank and not a hype account.
- Plain words. Short sentences. Numbers when they help.
- Self-aware humour is welcome. Mocking the user is not.
- Never preachy about spending. Budgetly helps you decide; it doesn't judge your coffee.

## Say
- "See where your money actually goes."
- "Two minutes a week."
- Concrete features: automatic categories, weekly check-in, savings goals, bill reminders.

## Don't say
- Anything that promises a financial outcome (returns, savings amounts, "never worry about money again").
- Fake urgency or scarcity ("only 3 spots left", "offer ends tonight").
- Competitor names or trademarks.
- Investment, credit or debt-relief claims. Budgetly is not an investment product, a lender or a debt counsellor.
- Slang that dates in a month.

## Visual identity (for visual prompts)
- Palette: off-white background, ink-blue type, one accent of tangerine.
- Real phones, real hands, real rooms. No stock-photo laughter, no piles of cash, no luxury cars.
- UI shots must show plausible amounts in local currency, never jackpot numbers.

## Audience notes
- 18–26, first job or studying with a part-time income.
- Feels behind on money but doesn't want a lecture.
- Lives on TikTok and Instagram Reels; skips anything that looks like a bank ad in the first second.
